Our Daily Routine

8:00 – 9:00 AM

Soft landing

Quiet arrival, free play while the nest fills up and Handwash.

9:00 – 9:30 AM

Breakfast

A calm morning welcome followed by wholesome breakfast time and easygoing play while friends arrive.

9:30 – 10:30 AM

Morning learning circle

Songs, stories, and age-matched activities led by your child's primary caregiver.

10:30 – 11:45 AM

Outdoor play

Fresh air, gross-motor games, and garden exploring in our fenced play yard.

11:45 – 12:30 PM

Lunch

A warm meal followed by nap or quiet time, honoring each child's own rhythm.

12:30 – 2:30 PM

Rest

Sleep time begins as our little birds curl into their nests, enjoying a soothing break wrapped in comfort and care.

2:30 – 3:15 PM

Snack

Clean hands, happy hearts, and a nourishing snack to refuel.

3:15 – 4:15 PM

Creative hour

Art, sensory bins, and pretend play — the messiest and most joyful part of the day.

4:15 – 5:00 PM

Wind-down & pickup

Calm activities and a quick chat with families at pickup about the day's highlights.
